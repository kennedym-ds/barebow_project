# Privacy Policy for BareTrack

**Effective Date:** February 22, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to BareTrack. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our Android and Desktop applications.

## 2. Data Collection and Storage
BareTrack is designed as a privacy-first, offline-first application. 
- **Local Storage:** All your data, including bow setups, arrow setups, session logs, and target face coordinates, is stored locally on your device using a SQLite database.
- **No Cloud Syncing:** We do not transmit, upload, or sync your personal data to any external servers or third-party cloud services.
- **No Tracking:** We do not use analytics trackers, crash reporters, or advertising SDKs that collect your personal information.

## 3. Android Auto Backup
If you have enabled Google Drive backups on your Android device, your local database (`baretrack.db`) may be automatically backed up to your personal Google Drive account by the Android operating system. This is controlled entirely by your device settings and Google account, and we do not have access to these backups.

## 4. Permissions
The BareTrack Android app requests the following permissions:
- **Internet:** Required strictly for the internal WebView to render the application interface. No external network requests are made by the app to transmit your data.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on this page.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository: [https://github.com/kennedym-ds/barebow_project](https://github.com/kennedym-ds/barebow_project).
